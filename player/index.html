Quick reference

Points are earned from objectives, role rewards and host/audience rewards. Spend them in the token shop.

Tokens create next-round effects. Shield/Immunity protect you, Guarantee/Double Shock target a player, and Blessing/Curse affect another player.

Hidden roles have their own progress and may award points, tokens or special next-round effects.

Pending actions may need host approval before they become active next-round effects.
